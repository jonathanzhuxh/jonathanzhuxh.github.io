My Story

我的故事

My name is Jonathan ZHU. Born in Shanghai in 1989, I now live in Singapore.

As a kid I was introverted and rarely went out to play. A few dog-eared encyclopedias at home were my entire world — one on world geography, one on natural science. I could spend a whole afternoon staring at a map, working out why a place was shaped the way it was. None of that ever turned into a certificate or a special skill, but it built a habit: before I believe anything or act on anything, I want to understand its structure and how it came to be first.

That habit went straight into my work. I spent twelve years in banking — starting from the most basic operational roles, and working my way up to independently designing data models and building automation systems. Honestly, I was never that interested in banking as an industry. What actually excites me is taking apart a messy process, finding the simplest possible fix, and getting it to run on its own — without needing me anymore. That's automation and digital transformation, in a sentence.

Outside of work, my interests are all over the place. History, politics, and geography run deep in me — I especially like obscure, cross-disciplinary topics, like the history of China's administrative divisions. I'm also a geek: phones, computers, rooting and flashing everything I could get my hands on, a hobby I only "retired" from in my forties. I'm a railway enthusiast too, and once spent half a month crossing Russia on the Trans-Siberian Railway. I've followed F1 and basketball for over a decade — more interested in the data and the tactics than the raw speed and adrenaline. I grew up on J-pop and followed idols, but even there my angle was different: I closely watched one woman convert her years as an idol into capital for a genuinely successful business career. None of this looks connected, but it's really the same habit wearing different clothes — I'm curious how any system works, and why it ended up shaped the way it did.

In 2025, for family reasons, we moved to Singapore, and I became a full-time stay-at-home dad. This gap has given me a chance to properly take stock of myself. I started two columns — one on the Singapore I see, one on the stories and history I've lived through. With the help of AI tools, I built this website myself and went back through the projects I'd worked on. I've always believed AI can't replace human judgment — but it does amplify your ideas and speed up the output. This website is itself an example of that — and, in its own way, just another version of taking a system apart to understand how it works.

我叫Jonathan Zhu，1989年生在上海，现在住在新加坡。

小时候我是个内向的孩子，并不怎么出门玩，家里有几本翻烂了的百科全书成了我的整个世界——一本讲各国地理，一本讲自然科学，我能一个下午就盯着地图看，琢磨一个地方为什么长成这样。这些东西后来没有变成什么特长证书，但养成了一种习惯：遇到任何事，我都想先搞清楚它的结构和来龙去脉，再决定要不要相信、要不要动手。

这个习惯后来直接用在了工作上。我在银行工作了12年，从最基础的业务操作做起，一路做到独立设计数据模型、搭建自动化系统。但说实话，我对银行这个行业本身兴趣并不大，真正让我兴致盎然的是拆解一套混乱的流程、找到最简单的解法、然后让它能自己转起来不再需要我的过程，也就是自动化和数字化转型。

工作之外，我的爱好很杂。我对历史政治和地理的爱好深入骨髓，特别喜欢研究一些冷门跨界专题，比如中国行政区划史。但同时我也是个Geek，手机电脑拆机刷机各种折腾，一路玩到中年才"退休"。我还是个铁路迷，曾用半个月乘坐西伯利亚大铁路横贯俄罗斯。我看了十几年F1和篮球，比起刺激感官的速度与激情，我更喜欢看数据和战术。我从小听JPop，也追过偶像，但我的关注点依然与众不同：我全程观察了一位成功通过变现偶像时期资产，创业成为企业家的女性。这些事似乎毫无关系，但其实都是同一个习惯的不同版本——我好奇任何一个系统是怎么运转的，以及它为什么会长成现在这样。

2025年，因为家庭原因，我们搬到了新加坡，我也成了全职奶爸。这段空档期让我有机会重新复盘一下自己。我写了两个专栏，一个记录我眼里的新加坡，一个记录我所经历的故事和历史。借着AI工具，我自己搭了这个网站，把过去做过的项目重新梳理了一遍。我一直觉得AI无法替代人做判断，但它会把一个人的想法放大，加快产出——这个网站本身就是个例子，也算是"拆解系统、搞清楚来龙去脉"的另一种玩法了。
